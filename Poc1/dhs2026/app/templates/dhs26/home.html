What is an Agentic Operating Layer?

The intelligent layer between Human and System

For decades, software has waited for instructions. Every workflow, click, and configuration has depended on a human operating the system one step at a time.

An Agentic Operating Layer changes that relationship. AI agents sit between human intent and the tools that get work done — understanding goals, coordinating across systems, and completing tasks end-to-end instead of waiting to be told what to click next.

Before

Manual software operation. Humans configure and orchestrate every step.

→
After

Agents understand intent, coordinate tools, and complete tasks end-to-end.
